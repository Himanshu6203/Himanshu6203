I’m passionate about turning **data into decisions**. I enjoy taking an ambiguous business problem, breaking it down into the right questions, analyzing the data, and translating the findings into insights that can actually drive action.

My work focuses on **Data Analytics, Business Analysis, and Product Analytics**, with hands-on experience across **SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI, and DAX**. I’m particularly interested in customer behavior, churn, segmentation, KPI analysis, and understanding what drives business performance.

I believe good analytics is more than building dashboards or writing queries — it’s about connecting **business questions → analysis → insights → recommendations**.

Currently, I’m deepening this approach through projects around **Customer Lifetime Value, Retention Strategy, and Customer Analytics**, where I combine technical analysis with business thinking to solve real-world problems.
